The boundary

Auger Math keeps drilling-time, burial-fraction and concrete arithmetic only. It gives no verdicts about what any auger can bite through - soil, roots and rock are the rental counter's and the manual's call. Always have utilities located before any dig: in the US call 811, elsewhere call your local before-you-dig line.

The sticker promises thirty seconds a hole. The fence line has forty of them, and the concrete truck only comes once. Auger Math does the honest arithmetic: how long the drilling really runs, how much of the post belongs underground, and how many bags the holes actually drink.

Three panels. The hole truth multiplies holes by the claimed seconds. The depth truth subtracts the above-ground height and judges the buried fraction against the one-third rule. The mix truth turns hole dimensions into bags of concrete.
